Our Story

Global Expertise, Local Execution

Jianan Engineering Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its corporate office in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. As an associate of Jianan Engineering Co., we bring international engineering expertise to the Indian market.

We deliver turnkey, EPC and EPCM projects with a strong focus on good engineering practices, disciplined execution and quality assurance. Our design and engineering capabilities are supported by modern computer-aided tools, enabling reliable delivery and lasting client satisfaction.
